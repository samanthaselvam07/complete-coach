Professional Bio

With over 12 years of experience in high-performance sports and specialized metabolic conditioning, Marcus has carved a unique space in the coaching world. His philosophy, The Kinetic Curator, focuses on the meticulous selection of movement patterns that maximize output while minimizing systemic fatigue.

Formerly a strength coach for elite European cycling teams, he transitioned to private coaching to bring world-class methodologies to ambitious professionals. Marcus believes that every athlete is a puzzle of biomechanics and psychological readiness, requiring a bespoke blueprint rather than a generic template.
